Effective Date: February 2, 2026

INTRODUCTION
We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how Autonomous
Diary handles your data.

DATA COLLECTION
Autonomous Diary:
  ✓ DOES NOT collect any personal data
  ✓ DOES NOT track your usage
  ✓ DOES NOT send data to external servers
  ✓ DOES NOT use analytics or telemetry
  ✓ DOES NOT contain ads

LOCAL STORAGE
All your diary entries and data are stored locally on your computer in the
diary_data/ folder. You have complete control over this data.

BACKUPS
You can backup your data by copying the diary_data/ folder to any location
you choose.

DELETIONS
You can delete your data at any time by deleting the diary_data/ folder.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES
This application does not use any third-party services, APIs, or libraries
that could compromise your privacy.

CONTACT
For privacy questions, contact us at: diary@personalsoftware.dev

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. We will notify users
of any significant changes.
