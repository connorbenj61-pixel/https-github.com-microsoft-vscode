STEP 1️⃣: REGISTER MICROSOFT PARTNER ACCOUNT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Go to: https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard/

1. Click "Register" (top right)
2. Sign in with Microsoft account
   - Create one if you don't have it
   - Use professional email (preferred)
3. Choose: "I want to create a new account"
4. Select: "Individual developer" or "Company"
5. Pay registration fee: $19 USD (one-time)
6. Complete identity verification
   - Provide valid payment method
   - Verify email and phone
7. Wait for approval (usually instant-24 hours)
8. Access Partner Dashboard once approved

TIME: 10-15 minutes
COST: $19 USD
