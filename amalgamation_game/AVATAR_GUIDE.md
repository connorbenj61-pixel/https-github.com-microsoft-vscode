# ROYAL AVATAR SYSTEM - MEDICAL HEALER KNIGHT

## Overview

The Royal Avatar System integrates your character (young girl in gold/silver enchanted steel armor) into the Amalgamation Game as a fully-fledged medical healer knight with progressive upgrade mechanics.

---

## Avatar Characteristics

### Physical Appearance
- **Age**: Young (child character)
- **Armor**: Enchanted Steel with gold and silver filigree
- **Cape**: Deep red royal cloak
- **Build**: Small but mighty knight warrior
- **Aesthetic**: Royal healer class with protective guardian gear

### Base Stats
| Stat | Value | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Health | 150 HP | Moderate for support class |
| Mana | 100 MP | For spell casting |
| Armor Class | 18 AC | High defense (D&D equivalent) |
| Attack | 12 | Moderate offensive capability |
| Defense | 15 | Strong defensive capability |
| Magical Power | 18 | High spellcasting stat |

## Medical Specialization System

### Six Medical Specialties

1. **Battlefield Medic** (Starting)
   - Focus: Quick, reactive healing
   - Strength: Fast casting
   - Weakness: Lower healing amounts
   - Signature: "First Aid" ability

2. **Alchemist**
   - Focus: Potion creation and crafting
   - Strength: Item-based healing
   - Weakness: Requires resources
   - Signature: "Poison Purge" ability

3. **Chirurgeon**
   - Focus: Precise surgical healing
   - Strength: Targeted healing
   - Weakness: Higher mana cost
   - Signature: Surgical techniques

4. **Healer Saint**
   - Focus: Holy restoration magic
   - Strength: Divine blessings
   - Weakness: Requires divine favor
   - Signature: "Grand Healing" ability

5. **Plague Doctor**
   - Focus: Disease and poison cure
   - Strength: Condition removal
   - Weakness: Limited healing
   - Signature: "Plague Remedy" ability

6. **Physician**
   - Focus: Balanced general medicine
   - Strength: Versatility
   - Weakness: Jack-of-all-trades
   - Signature: "Minor Restoration" ability

### Switching Specialties
- Change medical specialty at any time
- Unlock new abilities with each tier
- Maintain mastery experience across specialties
- Respec available through in-game mechanics

## Medical Tier Progression

Five-tier advancement system for medical skill:

### Tier 1: Initiate
- **Requirements**: Starting tier
- **Abilities Unlocked**:
  - First Aid (20 HP healing, 15 mana)
  - Minor Restoration (15 HP healing, 10 mana)
  - Poison Purge (remove toxins, 20 mana)
- **Spellcasting Bonus**: None

### Tier 2: Apprentice
- **Requirements**: 100+ medical experience
- **Abilities Unlocked**:
  - Improved Restoration (35 HP healing, 25 mana)
  - Better Potion Crafting
- **Spellcasting Bonus**: +10% healing

### Tier 3: Journeyman
- **Requirements**: 300+ medical experience
- **Abilities Unlocked**:
  - Specialty Mastery (50 HP, specialty-dependent)
  - Enhanced Status Effects
- **Spellcasting Bonus**: +20% healing, -10% mana cost

### Tier 4: Master
- **Requirements**: 600+ medical experience
- **Abilities Unlocked**:
  - Grand Healing (75 HP healing, 50 mana)
  - Multiple Targets
- **Spellcasting Bonus**: +30% healing, -20% mana cost

### Tier 5: Legendary
- **Requirements**: 1000+ medical experience
- **Abilities Unlocked**:
  - Mythic Restoration (100 HP healing, 75 mana)
  - Permanent Blessings
  - Full Resurrection (costs all mana)
- **Spellcasting Bonus**: +40% healing, -30% mana cost

## Healing Abilities

### Ability System Overview

Each ability has:
- **Healing Power**: Base HP restored
- **Mana Cost**: Resource to cast
- **Cooldown**: Turns before recast
- **Tier Requirement**: Minimum medical tier
- **Specialty**: Which specialty it belongs to

### Starter Abilities

#### First Aid
```
Specialty: Battlefield Medic
Healing: 20 HP (+ variance: -5 to +10)
Mana Cost: 15
Cooldown: 1 turn
Description: "Quick battlefield healing for self or ally"
Perfect For: Emergency healing, low-cost option
```

#### Minor Restoration
```
Specialty: Physician
Healing: 15 HP (+ variance)
Mana Cost: 10
Cooldown: 2 turns
Description: "Basic health restoration"
Perfect For: Regular maintenance healing
```

#### Poison Purge
```
Specialty: Alchemist
Effect: Remove all toxins/poisons
Mana Cost: 20
Cooldown: 2 turns
Description: "Remove poisoning and toxins"
Perfect For: Condition removal, support
```

### Unlockable Abilities

#### Improved Restoration (Tier 2)
- Healing: 35 HP
- Mana Cost: 25
- Cooldown: 2 turns
- Enhanced version of Minor Restoration

#### Specialty Mastery (Tier 3)
- Healing: 50 HP
- Mana Cost: 35
- Cooldown: 3 turns

- Strongest single-target heal early game

#### Grand Healing (Tier 4)
- Healing: 75 HP
- Mana Cost: 50
- Cooldown: 4 turns
- Multi-target capable
- Can heal multiple allies

#### Mythic Restoration (Tier 5)
- Healing: 100 HP
- Mana Cost: 75
- Cooldown: 5 turns
- Ultimate healing power
- Can restore fallen allies

## Healing Inventory

### Starting Inventory
- **Healing Potions**: 5
  - Effect: 30-50 HP restoration
  - Cost: None (consumable item)
  - Reusable: Can be restocked
  
- **Antidotes**: 3
  - Effect: Remove single poison/disease
  - Cost: None (consumable item)
  - Reusable: Can be restocked
  
- **Restoration Scrolls**: 2
  - Effect: Restore mana or health based on scroll type
  - Cost: None (consumable item)
  - Reusable: Can be restocked

### Inventory Management
- Use items without mana cost
- Limited quantity per game
- Restock between battles
- Trade items for better ones with progression

### Potion Types (Expandable)
- **Health Potion**: +30-50 HP
- **Greater Health Potion**: +50-75 HP (higher tier)
- **Mana Potion**: +30-50 mana
- **Cure-All Potion**: Remove all conditions

## Status System

### Conditions (Negative Effects)
Avatar can suffer from:
- **Poisoned**: Damage over time, reduced healing effectiveness
- **Bleeding**: HP loss each turn
- **Stunned**: Cannot act next turn
- **Weakened**: Reduced damage output
- **Cursed**: Negative spell effects
- **Frozen**: Movement restricted

### Buffs (Positive Effects)
Avatar can gain:
- **Protected**: Increased defense
- **Strengthened**: Increased attack
- **Holy Blessed**: Healing bonus
- **Enhanced Mana**: Increased mana pool
- **Regeneration**: Passive HP healing
- **Magical Focus**: Increased spell power

### Buff/Debuff Management
- Active tracking of all conditions
- Remove conditions with abilities
- Stack multiple buffs
- Buffs can override conditions
- Tracked in avatar display

## Avatar in Gameplay

### How Avatar Works in Battles

1. **Pre-Battle Preparation**
   - Select healing specialty
   - Choose loadout of abilities
   - Prepare inventory (potions, scrolls)

2. **During Battle**
   - Cast healing spells on self or allies
   - Use potions for emergency healing
   - Manage mana resources
   - Cure conditions from enemies

3. **After Battle**
   - Recover mana and health
   - Gain medical experience
   - Inventory restocked (in safe locations)

4. **Between Battles**
   - Upgrade medical tier
   - Learn new specialties
   - Craft new potions
   - Rest and prepare

### Avatar Abilities in Tournament Mode

In tournament battles against AI opponents:
- Use healing to sustain yourself
- Support allies (if available)
- Adapt specialty to opponent weaknesses
- Manage resources across multiple rounds
