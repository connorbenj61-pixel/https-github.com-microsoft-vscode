🎮 HOW TO PLAY
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

STARTING THE GAME:

1. Launch: Genius3DChess.exe
2. Click: "🎮 PLAY vs AI"
3. Select: Your opponent (choose by skill level)
4. Play: You are WHITE (bottom of board)

MAKING MOVES:

1. Click a WHITE piece to select it
   → Selected piece highlights in YELLOW
   → Valid moves show in GREEN

2. Click a GREEN square to move there
   → Piece moves to destination
   → If opponent piece is there, it's captured ♔

3. Wait for opponent's turn
   → Watch game log for opponent's commentary
   → Opponent makes its move automatically

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
♟ PIECE MOVEMENTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PAWN (♙)
├─ Moves: 1 square forward (2 on first move)
├─ Captures: 1 square diagonally forward
├─ Special: Promotes to Queen at end of board
└─ Strategy: Use as shield, advance to promote

KNIGHT (♘)
├─ Moves: L-shaped (2 squares + 1 square at right angle)
├─ Captures: Same movement pattern
├─ Special: Only piece that jumps over others
└─ Strategy: Great for attacking from unexpected angles

BISHOP (♗)
├─ Moves: Diagonally any number of squares
├─ Captures: Same movement
├─ Special: Stays on same color squares (white/black)
└─ Strategy: Control long diagonals, pair for power

ROOK (♖)
├─ Moves: Horizontally or vertically any number of squares
├─ Captures: Same movement
├─ Special: Very powerful in endgame
└─ Strategy: Control open files and ranks

QUEEN (♕)
├─ Moves: Any direction any number of squares
├─ Captures: Same movement
├─ Special: Combination of rook + bishop
└─ Strategy: Most powerful, protect well!

KING (♔)
├─ Moves: 1 square in any direction
├─ Captures: Same movement
├─ Special: Game ends if checkmated
└─ Strategy: Keep safe in early/middle game

🎯 STRATEGY TIPS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OPENING PRINCIPLES:
✓ Control center (e4, d4, e5, d5 squares)
✓ Develop pieces quickly (move knights and bishops out)
✓ Don't move queen too early (vulnerable to attacks)
✓ Castle early (move king to safety)
✓ Connect your rooks (create a strong back rank)

MIDDLE GAME:
✓ Look 3-4 moves ahead
✓ Protect your pieces
✓ Attack undefended opponent pieces
✓ Create threats (multiple attacks)
✓ Control key squares
✓ Don't leave pieces hanging (undefended)

ENDGAME:
✓ Trade pieces when ahead
✓ Activate your king (it becomes strong)
✓ Advance pawns toward promotion
✓ Use rooks on the 7th rank
✓ Create passed pawns (pawns nothing can stop)

TACTICAL MOTIFS:
• Fork: Attack two pieces at once with one piece
• Pin: Attack piece that can't move (behind it is more valuable)
• Skewer: Reverse of pin (valuable piece moves, exposing lower piece)
• Discovered Attack: Move piece, revealing attack from piece behind it
• Back Rank Mate: Attack king trapped on its back rank

🎯 CHESS TIPS & TRICKS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BASIC PRINCIPLES:
1. Protect Your King
   └─ Castle (move king to safety) after moving 2-3 pieces

2. Develop Pieces
   └─ Move knights and bishops out before queen

3. Control Center
   └─ Fight for center 4 squares (d4, e4, d5, e5)

4. Don't Hang Pieces
   └─ Never leave piece undefended where it can be taken

5. Look Ahead
   └─ Before moving, check: Can opponent capture? What's his response?

TACTICAL PATTERNS:
• Two Bishops: Very strong in endgame, attack both diagonals
• Knight and Bishop: Beautiful combination, strong attacks
• Rook and Queen: Devastating together, mate threats
• Passed Pawn: Push toward promotion, hard to stop

POSITION EVALUATION:
Good Signs:
✓ Pieces actively placed and protected
✓ Opponent king exposed or weak
✓ Pawn structure solid (no holes)
✓ Piece coordination (pieces work together)
✓ More valuable pieces than opponent

Bad Signs:
✗ Pieces passive or attacked
✗ King position weak
✗ Pawn structure broken
✗ Pieces scattered (don't work together)
✗ Fewer valuable pieces
