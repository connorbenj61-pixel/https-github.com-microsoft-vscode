Maternity Support & Return-to-Work

Home Maternity Support Resources

Core considerations

  • Flexible rehabilitation timelines for parents (pregnancy, postpartum recovery, breastfeeding)
  • Childcare support options during therapy, training, and appointments
  • Phased return-to-work plans with reasonable adjustments and reduced hours
  • Respect privacy, avoid mandatory disclosure of medical details beyond necessity

Programs and supports

Examples (local availability varies):

  • Maternity-aware counseling and peer support groups for healthcare workers
  • Vocational retraining that accommodates parenting schedules
  • Legal advice regarding parental leave, discrimination, and return-to-work rights

Building a maternity-friendly pathway

  1. Assess clinical and practical needs with sensitivity to parenting status.
  2. Offer childcare stipends, recorded sessions, or remote/telehealth options.
  3. Create clear, documented phased return plans and point-of-contact for adjustments.
