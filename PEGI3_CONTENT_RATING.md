## Rating Summary

ArmourboundGuardianAI is a **PEGI 3 rated** interactive learning and planning system suitable for all ages, including very young children.

### What is PEGI 3?

PEGI 3 is a rating that indicates content is suitable for ages 3+. At this level:
- ✅ No violence or scary content
- ✅ No bad language or adult themes
- ✅ Content is educational and positive
- ✅ Age-appropriate storytelling and learning
- ✅ Safe for children to use unsupervised

## Parental Guidance Notes

### For Parents of Young Children (Ages 3-6)
ArmourboundGuardianAI is a safe, educational tool featuring:
- Fun facts about animals (dolphins, dinosaurs)
- Storybook recommendations
- Simple space exploration concepts
- Interactive learning games and adventures

**Suggested use:** Co-engage with your child. Read the content together, talk about the topics, and enjoy the learning experience.

### For Older Children (Ages 7+)
The system offers more complex learning paths including:
- Advanced domain learning
- Space mission planning concepts
- Historical and scientific topics
- Multi-step problem solving

**Suggested use:** Allow independent exploration with occasional check-ins about what they're learning.

### For Educators
ArmourboundGuardianAI provides structured learning pathways suitable for:
- Classroom supplementary material
- Homeschooling reference
- STEM education introduction
- Creative writing inspiration
